Sound credits

Keylab uses the tidal-drum-machines collection used by Strudel. Samples stream directly from that repository; they are not bundled with this app.

The Roland TR-909 kit uses its RolandTR909 samples. The acoustic kit uses Boss DR-660 “Real” kick, snare, hi-hat and tom samples, with its clap and crash. This is a sampled acoustic-style kit. Instrument names belong to their respective owners; Keylab is not affiliated with Roland.

Sine, triangle, square, and sawtooth are generated with the Web Audio API. Supersaw layers three detuned sawtooth oscillators. Synths are similar in character to Strudel; synthesis envelopes and output processing can differ.

Strudel exports explicitly load the same drum sample URLs to preserve the selected sound. External sample access requires an internet connection. First-time audio setup preloads every drum before recording.
